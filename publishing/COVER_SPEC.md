# Cover specification

Exactly two final cover files are allowed:

1. `cover-horizontal-4x3.png` — 1600×1200;
2. `cover-vertical-3x4.png` — 1200×1600.

Both reuse the approved Chen Yan and riverside-girl identity anchors, rain-blue/sodium-orange palette, ink contour and wardrobe from the episode. Generated artwork contains no text, logo, watermark, red guide geometry or UI. Local typography adds these exact strings:

- Primary: `高考回档`
- Secondary: `雨夜白月光`
- Hook: `426分后，我回到了三天前`

The protagonist is the dominant focal point; the girl is a distant secondary silhouette/reaction, never sexualized and never placed in a graphic self-harm pose. Leave deliberate negative space for the title. The two ratios are separately composed from the same IP, not one stretched into the other.
